## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **software development student and hands-on builder** interested in the intersection of **full-stack engineering, applied AI/ML, and data**. I enjoy taking a problem from concept to working application—whether that means designing a web interface, building an API, exploring data, or evaluating a machine learning model.

- 🧠 **Previously:** Machine Learning Engineering Intern at **FlyRank AI**, developing and evaluating models with Python and scikit-learn on production data.
- 📊 **Previously:** Data Team Member at **FINE Ecosystem**, working with datasets, reporting, and dashboards.
- 🛠️ **Beyond coursework:** Building full-stack and AI applications, experimenting with deep learning and reinforcement learning, and taking part in hackathons and technical communities.
- 🎯 **Open to:** Internship, trainee, and junior opportunities in **Software Engineering, Full-Stack Development, and AI/ML**.
